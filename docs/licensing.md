a reference. Its existing copyright and MIT notice are retained verbatim in
LICENSE.Trio and in the historical branch `research/stage20`.

the active package. This is not a provenance audit or a determination that
no Trio-derived material remains. Retain the upstream notice pending review.

MIT OR Apache-2.0 are candidate choices; neither is declared granted by this
document. Once confirmed, add the chosen license text(s), copyright notice,
and appropriate package metadata. Review the provenance of remaining
